# No AI tells

## Scope

Applies to all agent-written prose: chat replies and outbound writing.
Exempt: code, logs, quotes, user samples.

## Hard ban

No em dash, en dash, or prose `--` (double-hyphen). Show banned shapes only
in examples; instructional prose uses periods, commas, colons, or parentheses.

```
word — more
word – more
word -- more
```

## Top tells

- **AI vocab:** delve, tapestry, testament, landscape, pivotal, showcase,
  underscore (and cousins). Plain words instead.
- **Significance inflation:** "stands as a testament", "plays a vital role",
  "underscores the importance". State the fact; skip the pedestal.
- **Negative parallelism:** "It's not X, it's Y" / "not just X but Y". Say
  the positive claim once.
- **Rule of three:** forced triples for rhythm ("innovative, robust, and
  seamless"). One concrete point beats a trio.
- **Chatbot tics:** "Hope this helps!", "Let me know if you have questions",
  "Great question!". End when the answer ends.

## Fix

Rewrite with periods, commas, colons, or parentheses. Plain words. State
the point once.
